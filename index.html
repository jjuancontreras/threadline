Chapter 1

Perch

Dusk bleeds out over the city. High on the needle of the Meridian Spire, a figure in blue-black crouches at the corner, a red skull-spider emblem glowing on the chest and red eyes burning in the mask.

They call the runner THREADRUNNER. Below, ten thousand windows switch on, one floor at a time.

Chapter 2

Swing

A step off the corner into nothing. The first tether snaps out, a red line of light that locks onto a tower's face, and gravity turns into speed.

Fire, dive, release, flip. Again and again, THREADRUNNER rides the canyon of glass toward the elevated maglev line.

Chapter 3

Threat

Two tethers, pulled back like a slingshot, fling THREADRUNNER onto a rooftop. Then a star in the sky starts to grow. A carrier the size of the city slides in overhead and its bay doors open.

GANTRY, a hulking yellow-and-rust demolition mech with one red eye, drops onto a roof hard enough to crack it. All across the skyline, more units in other colours come down after it.

Chapter 4

Impact

Hydraulics howl. GANTRY tears a steel cargo container off the roof and hurls it, and for one stretched heartbeat time almost stops.

Then it hits. The track shears apart, and a section of the bridge drops into the street. The train is still coming, fast.

Chapter 5

Rescue

THREADRUNNER lands on the lead car and turns back toward the towers. Click or tap the glowing anchors, or tab to them and press Enter, to fire tethers.

Each line bites and strains, and the train slows. It stops a hand's width from the edge. The train is saved.

Epilogue

The line holds

Sirens rise from the streets below. The carrier lifts back into the clouds, and on its rooftop GANTRY's red eye goes dark. THREADRUNNER is already gone, a thin red line fading between the towers.
